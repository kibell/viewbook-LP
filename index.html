**The Applied Bachelor of Science in Business Administration at Southern New Hampshire University (SNHU) is a 93-credit, three-year (six-semester) program designed to provide students with an accelerated bachelor’s degree. This reduced-time and reduced-credit program has been approved by SNHU’s institutional accreditor, the New England Commission of Higher Education (NECHE). However, the standard baccalaureate degree remains a four-year (eight-semester), 120-credit program. Students who choose this pathway can complete both their bachelor’s degree and MBA in just four years*. This program meets admission requirements for SNHU master’s degrees, but students should be aware that other institutions may have different admission criteria. Prospective graduate students are encouraged to verify specific requirements with their intended institutions. The Applied Bachelor of Science in Business Administration includes learning experiences with translatable skills to help build a solid resume and prepare graduates for the workforce. However, as this is a novel program structure, students should be aware that some employers may have varying perspectives on a three-year degree. * Dependent on students maintaining full time enrollment in both the Applied Bachelor of Science in Business Administration and the MBA.

Student support like no other

We surround each student with a constellation of support that includes academics and advising, accessibility, career services, nutrition, and health and wellness, including counseling. At SNHU, you’re one of us — and you never have to face any situation alone.

  • Academic Advising
    Your advisor is here to help. Explore opportunities and overcome challenges on the way to your degree together.
  • Career Development Center
    You're here to prepare for what's next. From resume reviews to interview practice, get help navigating your next step.
  • Office of Diversity Programs
    You belong at SNHU. That's why we're building a diverse and inclusive community to help you fit in while still standing out.
  • Wellness Center
    Your wellbeing matters. With our licensed health center, you can get free, confidential general and mental health support.
  • Veterans Lounge
    We support those who've served. You can use this space to relax, receive guidance and find your community.
  • Campus Accessibility Center
    We believe education is for everyone. Whether an accommodation is physical or academic, we're here to help make the path to a degree possible.
  • Wolak Learning Center
    Learning how to learn is part of the journey. This hub connects you to tutoring, mentoring and study skill seminars.
  • Student Financial Services
    Going to college is an investment in you. Get info on grants, loans and payment options to help pay for your degree.
  • Dining Services
    You have a lot on your plate. So take a break and join your friends in the dining hall or stop by our (brand new!) Dunkin' and Einstein Bros.
  • Debra L. Coffin Women’s Center
    We're curating a safe space for all. Programming and conversations on gender-related social issues flourish here.
  • International Student Services
    College can seem like a whole new world. Here, you can meet others and get advice on cultural, language and immigration topics.

SNHU celebrates our first-generation college students!

Since 1932, Southern New Hampshire University's mission has been to expand access to high-quality, affordable education. In fact, many of our faculty and staff are first-generation college students!

If neither of your parents, nor anyone who raised you, has graduated with a bachelor's degree, you’re a first-generation college student — and we’re thrilled you’re applying to SNHU!

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, it's free to apply to SNHU on the Common App. As part of our mission to increase access to higher education, we do not charge application fees for U.S. residents.
SNHU is a nonprofit, 501(c)(3) institution with a mission — your success. According to the National Center for Education Statistics, students who pursue their bachelor's degree at private nonprofit institutions have much higher graduation rates than their counterparts at for-profit schools.

SNHU is a private, fully accredited institution. As a private university, we rely heavily on private donations, contributions and tuition to provide nationally recognized education to our students. SNHU offers countless resources to ensure student success, including, but not limited to, merit-based scholarship programs, dedicated advisor support and lifetime access to career services for alumni.
No, SNHU does not require scores on standardized tests like the SAT, ACT, GMAT or GRE. We believe that these tests do not effectively measure a student's subject knowledge or their ability to succeed.
No. SNHU doesn't require supplemental essays for admission through the Common App, but we do require a personal essay as part of the individual section on the Common App.
SNHU’s 300-acre campus is situated at 2500 North River Road in Manchester, New Hampshire — just minutes from downtown Manchester and about an hour from the city of Boston, the seacoast and the mountain region of New Hampshire.

We invite you to schedule a campus tour, available on both weekdays and weekends throughout the year.
There are approximately 2,800 undergraduate students attending the SNHU campus. 64% of these undergraduates live on campus, with the remainder commuting from surrounding New England communities.

Fun fact: Our students come from 37 U.S. states and 48 countries!
The average grade-point average (GPA) for the incoming 2024 undergraduate class is 3.25.
Yes, Southern New Hampshire University is institutionally accredited by the New England Commission of Higher Education, Inc. (NECHE). All programs offered by SNHU are also encompassed by this regional institutional accreditation.

Programmatic Accreditations

In addition to having regional accreditation, certain SNHU undergraduate and graduate programs also carry specialized programmatic accreditations, including:

  • Commission on Accreditation for Health Informatics and Information Management Education (CAHIIM)
  • Commission on Collegiate Nursing Education (CCNE)
  • Council for Accreditation of Counseling and Related Educational Programs (CACREP)
  • Council for the Accreditation of Educator Preparation (CAEP)
  • Council on Education for Public Health (CEPH)
  • Engineering Accreditation Commission of ABET (Accreditation Board for Engineering and Technology)
  • New Hampshire State Department of Education for Teacher Certification
